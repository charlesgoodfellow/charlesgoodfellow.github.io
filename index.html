01 About

I like to think of myself as a talented but humble technology professional. Today I'm a Site Reliability Engineer at WaFd Bank, where I focus on observability: shaping the monitoring strategy for a hybrid cloud and on-prem environment, and helping application teams see what their systems are really doing.

Before WaFd, I was a Systems Engineer on the Starbucks Connect platform team, building tooling and automation for endpoint management, mostly in PowerShell. Earlier still, I worked in systems administration, IT support, and field services for iconic Seattle-area organizations like Brown Bear and Goodwill, as well as larger global companies.

Technology is fun, but my favorite part of the job is the people: the customers, co-workers, and leaders I've met along the way. People are always the reason. If you want to talk tech, or you're looking for an engineer to join your team, reach out on LinkedIn. I'm always glad to make a new connection.
